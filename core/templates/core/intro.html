About & Help

Welcome to MediumClone — a Medium-like blog built with Django for learning purposes.

How to use

  • Create an account or log in to write and save stories.
  • Use the editor toolbar to add headings, links, images and videos.
  • Press “Save” on a story to keep it in your library, or add it to a reading list.
  • Follow writers to read their member-only stories and get notified about new posts.

Contact & Support

If you need help, open an issue in the project repository.
